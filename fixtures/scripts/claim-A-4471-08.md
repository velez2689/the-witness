# Demo corpus — claim A-4471-08

Six calls on one claim, June to August. Five are history; **call 06 is the live call the demo runs.**
All names, IDs, reference numbers and payer are invented. **No PHI. No real payer. No CPT descriptors.**

Every line is scripted for text-to-speech. Each call is rendered twice: **clean** and **degraded**
(hold music bleed, compression, one overlap). The degraded set is what the demo plays.
